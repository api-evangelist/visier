The headlines say AI is eliminating software jobs. Visier's analysis of 3.6 million employee records—paired with employee interviews— tells a different story: roles are despecializing and reorganizing, and the real costs (mentorship, team cohesion, skill atrophy) don't show up in headcount data at all.
