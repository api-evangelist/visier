HR data is unlike almost any other data your organization holds—volatile, legally sensitive, and deeply contextual. Most AI deployments treat it like any other data source and pay the price. Here's what the architecture actually needs: a context layer, durable guardrails, and a semantic foundation that travels with the data wherever it goes.
